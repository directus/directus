Updated Unhead from 1.11.20 to 3.4.1

::: notice

`@directus/themes` now requires `@unhead/vue` 3.x as a peer dependency. Projects that pair it with their own Unhead instance need to upgrade and follow the [v2](https://unhead.unjs.io/docs/migration-guide/v2) and [v3](https://unhead.unjs.io/docs/vue/migration-guide/v3) migration guides.

:::
